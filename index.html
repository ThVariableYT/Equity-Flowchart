What is an Equity Advisory?

An Equity Advisory is a specialized financial practice providing registered, independent research and customized portfolio strategy directly to individual and institutional investors.

100% Non-Discretionary

Unlike asset managers or mutual funds, you maintain total ownership of your assets. The advisory publishes institutional-grade model baskets, rebalance alerts, and entry bands—but you execute the trades yourself. Your money never leaves your licensed brokerage account.

Zero Custody Exposure

Fiduciary Standard

Traditional stockbrokers operate under a "suitability" standard and generate revenue through transaction churn. Registered Investment Advisers (RIAs) are bound by the legal fiduciary standard, obligated to place client capital preservation and growth above all else.

SEBI / SEC Compliant

Zero Commissions

Fee-only architecture eliminates conflicts of interest. The advisory charges either a predictable flat subscription or an explicit % of Assets Under Advice (AUA). No broker rebates, no distributor cuts, and zero incentive to over-trade your account.

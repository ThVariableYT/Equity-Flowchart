Fiduciary Standard

What is an Equity Advisory?

An independent, SEBI-registered advisory service that equips you with institutional research and tailored portfolio strategies without taking custody of your funds or executing trades without your explicit approval.

100% Client Demat Custody

Your securities are held directly in your personal Demat account with NSDL or CDSL. PhillipCapital Advisory never pools client funds, eliminating counterparty custody risk.

Zero Custody Exposure

Fee-Only Fiduciary Model

Regulated under SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013. We do not accept commissions, kickbacks, or distribution incentives from listed entities or exchanges.

Zero Trade Churn Conflict

Phillip Institutional Pedigree

Founded in Singapore in 1975, PhillipCapital operates across 15 countries with over USD 35 Billion in assets under custody and management, bringing global research rigor to Indian equities.

49+ Years of Institutional Trust
